## Brand & Style
The design system operates on the ethos of cryptographic rigor, architectural cartography, and archival permanence. It rejects decorative softness, skeuomorphic gradients, and marketing fluff in favor of structural clarity, high-density data parsing, and technical precision.

The emotional signature is cool, calculating, and authoritative. Interfaces built with this system should feel like specialized instrumentation—a cross between high-security cryptographic infrastructure and precision aerospace blueprints. 

Visually, the system marries Technical Brutalism with Monastic Precision:
- Structural wireframe grids with exposed, deliberate bounding lines.
- Dense monospaced data alignment balanced by disciplined typographic hierarchies.
- Strict functional containment where every panel, frame, and divider signifies an explicit data boundary.

## Colors
The palette is built from an obsidian and graphite bedrock, punctuated by precision-milled metal borders and cool architectural cyan-cobalt inks.

### Canvas & Surface Layers
- **Obsidian Base Canvas:** `#0D1117` — Ground level for the viewport and primary document backdrops.
- **Deep Graphite Surface:** `#161B22` — Default container and structural module background.
- **Elevated Graphite Surface:** `#21262D` — Active modules, hover states, and popovers.

### Structural Framework
- **Titanium Boundary / Grid Lines:** `#30363D` — Structural framing, 1px module borders, and architectural dividers.
- **Subtle Hairline Trace:** `#21262D` — Secondary subdivision grids, minor telemetry ticks, and quiet splitters.

### Ink & Contrast Scales
- **Cool Paper White (Primary):** `#F0F6FC` — Headlines, critical metrics, and verified states.
- **Ice-Steel Muted (Secondary):** `#C9D1D9` — Body copy, field labels, and secondary keys.
- **Sub-Titanium Ghost (Tertiary):** `#8B949E` — Timestamps, cryptographic hashes, line indices, metadata.

### Precision Inks (Telemetry & Actuation)
- **Cobalt Anchor (Interactive Accent):** `#388BFD` — Focused outlines, primary actuation controls, and verified badges.
- **Electric Cyan Ink (Telemetry / State):** `#58A6FF` — Active telemetry indicators, focus states, cryptographic confirmation pings.
- **Status Anchors (Monochrome-Tuned):**
  - Success / Attested: `#3FB950`
  - Warning / Delta: `#D29922`
  - Critical / Fault: `#F85149`

## Typography
Typographic rhythm is divided into three distinct roles:
1. **Architectural Display & Structural Headers (`Space Grotesk`):** Delivers clean geometry, crisp apexes, and drafting-table authority.
2. **Textual Body & Contextual Flow (`Inter`):** Neutral, hyper-legible, functional, and devoid of stylistic idiosyncrasies that could impair reading density.
3. **Telemetry, Identifiers & Data Readouts (`JetBrains Mono`):** Applied to all technical variables, values, hashes, badges, table cells, and status callouts.

All uppercase metadata labels use `JetBrains Mono` with tracking expanded between `0.08em` and `0.1em` to evoke technical documentation stamps and architectural title blocks. Tabular numbers (`tnum`) and slashed zeros are strictly enabled by default across all font stacks.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to an engineering coordinate framework based on a strict 4px/8px modular cadence. Layout boundaries are framed with explicit 1px titanium edges rather than expansive white space.

### Grid Construction
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column rigid grid with `gutter-lg` (2rem) and `margin-lg` (3rem). Panels snap directly to structural crosshairs.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `gutter-md` (1.5rem) and `margin-md` (2rem). Non-essential secondary columns collapse into vertical docked sheets.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column column structure with `gutter` (1rem) and `margin` (1rem). Multi-pane consoles stack into linear vertical modules with dividing hairline rules.

### Spatial Discipline
Whitespace is disciplined and functional, preventing wasted canvas area while ensuring structural demarcations are unmistakably clear. Internal component padding relies on `space-xs` and `space-sm` for dense data matrices, expanding to `space-md` and `space-lg` solely for architectural module shells.

## Elevation & Depth
This design system intentionally rejects diffuse ambient drop-shadows and soft skeuomorphic blurs. Depth is achieved strictly through **Tonal Strata** and **1px Titanium Precision Framing**.

### Surface Strata
- **Bedrock (Level 0):** `#0D1117` — The absolute foundation.
- **Structural Module (Level 1):** `#161B22` framed with a 1px border of `#30363D`.
- **Active Overlay / Flight Level (Level 2):** `#21262D` with a 1px border of `#388BFD` or `#30363D`. Used for command palettes, pinned side-drawers, and telemetry inspection panels.

### Contrast Boundaries
No floating surfaces are permitted without explicit boundary outlines. When an element floats over data (such as a modal or dropdown menu), it uses a 1px border of `#30363D` paired with an optional micro-hairline inner rim (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`). Modals are backed by an obsidian shield (`#0D1117` at 85% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`).

## Shapes
The shape language is strictly **Sharp (0px)** or **Micro-Chamfered**. Surfaces terminate in precise 90-degree right angles, mirroring technical drafting sheets, IC blueprints, and high-tensile milled alloy components. 

Pill shapes, playful soft radiuses, and floating bubbles are strictly forbidden. The geometry reinforces an unyielding standard of mathematical rigor and structural permanence.

## Components

### Buttons & Actuators
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#388BFD` fill, text `#F0F6FC`, font `JetBrains Mono` (0.75rem, uppercase, tracking `0.08em`), 0px border-radius, padding `0.5rem 1rem`. Active state shifts to `#58A6FF`.
- **Secondary / Archival:** Surface `#161B22`, 1px solid `#30363D` border, text `#C9D1D9`. On hover: surface transitions to `#21262D` and border illuminates to `#58A6FF`.
- **Danger / Abort:** Surface `#161B22`, 1px solid `#F85149` border, text `#F85149`. On hover: background shifts to `rgba(248, 81, 73, 0.15)`.

### Input Fields & Terminal Prompts
- Background `#0D1117`, 1px solid border `#30363D`, text `#F0F6FC`, font `JetBrains Mono` (0.875rem).
- Focus state: Border illuminates to `#58A6FF` with zero diffuse glow. An optional corner registration mark or left-edge indicator (2px solid `#58A6FF`) reinforces precision targeting.
- Label: Monospaced uppercase string (`label-sm`), placed strictly above or embedded into the upper border edge.

### Chips & Telemetry Tags
- Compact, rectangular badges with 0px radius.
- Background `#161B22`, border 1px solid `#30363D`, font `JetBrains Mono` (0.6875rem, uppercase).
- Status variants include a 6px x 6px square indicator (e.g., `#3FB950` for verified data, `#58A6FF` for active telemetry).

### Data Tables & Log Lists
- Rows bounded by 1px horizontal borders in `#21262D`.
- Alternating rows may utilize `#161B22` for high-density scanning.
- Column headers: JetBrains Mono (`label-sm`), uppercase, text `#8B949E`, terminated with a 1px solid `#30363D` border rule.
- Cell selection: 1px border ring in `#388BFD`.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Toggles
- **Checkbox:** Square box (14px x 14px), 0px radius, 1px solid border `#30363D`, background `#0D1117`. Selected state displays a sharp, filled cobalt square (8px x 8px) or clean crosshair.
- **Segmented Radio Group:** Linear structural rail. Selected option adopts `#21262D` background, `#F0F6FC` text, and a distinct top or bottom 2px `#58A6FF` anchor line.

### Cards & Modular Frames
- Enclosed by 1px solid `#30363D` borders over `#161B22` background.
- Standard header bar: Dedicated 28px tall sub-strip at top with `#21262D` background, 1px bottom border, and technical module title in `JetBrains Mono` accompanied by index identifiers (e.g., `SYS://01-TEL`).
- Optional technical accents: Crosshair indicators (`+`) at panel intersection corners.
