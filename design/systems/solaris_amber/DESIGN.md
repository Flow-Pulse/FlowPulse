## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an aerospace telemetry and mission-critical instrumentation aesthetic tailored for Stellar/Soroban smart contract infrastructure and open-source dependency streaming protocols. It communicates real-time precision, uninterrupted uptime, and structural durability.

### Personality & Tone
- **Instrumental & Operational:** Feels like an avionics heads-up display or space operations control desk—structured, data-dense, and utilitarian without visual clutter.
- **Mission-Critical Rigor:** High visual certainty. Every status indicator, metric readout, and streaming node connection exhibits unambiguous state definition.
- **Warm Industrial Energy:** High-temperature cadmium and solar golds puncture deep cold obsidian slates, conveying raw financial and protocol throughput.

### Visual Style
A calibrated synthesis of **technical instrumentation** and **refined dark-mode industrialism**:
- Deep layered obsidian slate substrates to minimize fatigue during long operational cycles.
- Hairline structural framing and low-contrast steel boundaries that emulate modular avionics racks.
- High-frequency data density paired with monospaced data readouts and uppercase micro-labels for immediate telemetry scanning.
- Radiant solar amber focal points for active execution paths, balances, and streaming liquidity vectors.

## Colors

The palette balances deep space darkness with radiant solar luminescence and high-legibility telemetry markers.

- **Primary Accent (Solar Amber & Cadmium):**
  - Primary Core: `#F59E0B` (Solar Amber - active streaming, core callouts)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy enforces immediate split-second scanning between conceptual navigation, functional telemetry data, and body-level system copy.

### Type Roles
- **Headlines & Metric Titles (Space Grotesk):** Provides mechanical, forward-leaning geometry suited for large financial values, Soroban contract addresses, and dashboard instrumentation titles.
- **System Interface & Narrative Body (Inter):** Highly neutral and legible for dense documentation, state logs, and user configuration panels.
- **Engineering Micro-Labels & Code Stream (JetBrains Mono):** Set with deliberate letter-spacing and uppercase tracking for system tags, dependency hashes, ledger sequence numbers, and stream rates.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an uninterrupted, data-dense fluid grid designed for low screen-waste in high-frequency monitoring interfaces.

### Grid & Density Hierarchy
- **Desktop (12-Column Modular Grid):** 24px gutters, 40px external canvas margin. Supports docked persistent telemetry drawers on the left/right flanks and a continuous central event canvas.
- **Tablet (8-Column Grid):** 16px gutters, 24px margins. Submodules compress into dual-column cards; sidebar menus transition into high-contrast utility shelves.
- **Mobile (4-Column Grid):** 16px gutters, 16px margins. Telemetry displays reflow from horizontal ticker arrays into stacked status modules.

### Internal Spacing Cadence
- **Compact Data Clusters:** Use `space-2xs` (2px) and `space-xs` (4px) for paired badge-to-label and metric-to-unit lockups.
- **Component Enclosures:** Standard component internal padding is locked to `space-sm` (8px) for compact toolbars and `space-md` (16px) for telemetry cards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tonal layering and crisp boundaries rather than heavy drop shadows to preserve telemetry contrast and eliminate visual mud.

### Surface Tiers & Layering
1. **Floor 0 (Canvas Substrate):** `#0B0E14`—Backing void containing raw gridlines.
2. **Tier 1 (Modular Panels):** `#11151E` with a 1px solid border of `#262D3D`. Used for static tables, telemetry grids, and dependency trees.
3. **Tier 2 (Interactive Modules):** `#181E29` with 1px `#262D3D` borders. Used for floating modals, popovers, and hovered table rows.
4. **Tier 3 (Active Focus / Execution Surfaces):** Background `#181E29` framed by a 1px `#D97706` border and an ambient amber wash.

### Phosphor Glow & Shadows
- **Standard Shadow:** Shadows are minimal. Panels use `0px 4px 16px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)` strictly for Z-axis separation of floating overlays.
- **Solar Amber Halo:** Primary active controls and live streaming sinks employ a localized, tight luminescence: `0px 0px 12px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.20)`.
- **Phosphor Telemetry Halo:** Positive ledger sync indicators use a green luminescent beacon: `0px 0px 8px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains precision geometry with a strict 8px foundational corner radius (ROUND_EIGHT).

### Geometry Rules
- **Modules, Cards & Containers:** Standardized at `0.5rem` (8px) border-radius to preserve structural, panelized aesthetics without feeling toy-like.
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Selects):** Standardized at `0.5rem` (8px), matching container edges for alignment.
- **Telemetry Tags & Micro-Badges:** Clipped at `0.25rem` (4px) to retain an angular microchip feel.
- **Circular Indicators:** Status pulses and avatar icons remain 50% circular, providing instant geometric contrast against the panel grid.

- **Dimensions:** 36px standard height for high density; 32px for micro/table-row triggers. Padding: 0 14px.

- **Geometry:** Height 22px, padding 2px 8px, border radius 4px, font `label-micro`.

### Cards & Panels
- **Anatomy:** Background `#11151E`, border 1px solid `#262D3D`, border-radius 8px.
- **Header Structure:** Border-bottom 1px solid `#1E2433`, padding 12px 16px, displaying panel title in `title-md` paired with a right-aligned telemetry indicator.
- **Body Section:** Padding 16px.

- **Precision Switch:** 34x18px rack-style track (`#181E29` border `#262D3D`). Indicator thumb: 12x12px square-pill (`#94A3B8`). Active state: track `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`, border `#F59E0B`, thumb `#F59E0B`.

### Lists & Data Grids
- **Rows:** Alternating subtle banding (transparent to `rgba(24, 30, 41, 0.3)`), border-bottom 1px solid `#1E2433`.
- **Header:** Sticky, background `#0B0E14`, text `label-micro` in `#94A3B8`, border-bottom 1px solid `#262D3D`.
- **Hover State:** Row background transitions to `#181E29` with the left border accent illuminating in `#F59E0B` (2px solid amber notch).

### Specialized Telemetry Components
- **Dependency Flow Node:** Modular block showing upstream crate/repo, live drip velocity (e.g., `+0.42 XLM/s`), and cryptographic checksum in monospaced chalk.
- **Contract Gas/Telemetry Fuel Gauge:** Segmented stepped horizontal bar in obsidian tracks, filled with sequential `#F59E0B` blocks turning `#84CC16` upon execution finality.
